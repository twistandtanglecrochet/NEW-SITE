Import products

Your 16 starter products live in the site's code, so they can't be edited here until you import them. The import tool copies them into this admin panel — after that you can edit, reorder or delete them like any other product. Products that are already imported are skipped, so it's safe to run again.

Open import tool

Add a new product

You can pick many photos at once (5 upload together). Click ☆ to make a photo the main one; use ◀ ▶ to change the order.
If you add variants, they replace the plain photo gallery on the site — customers tap a variant to switch its photo.

Prices & tags

Change the price and tag (New, Bestseller, Limited Stock, Sold Out) of many products at once, then press Save. Rows you changed turn yellow.

Tag “Sold Out” shows the Sold Out badge on the shop.

Stock is optional and only for you (customers don't see it). Warn me on the dashboard when a product has or fewer left.

Get a QR code to share

Send this to customers who order from Instagram or Facebook so they can view the product and leave a review. Pick "Whole shop" for one QR code you reuse for every order, or pick a specific product for a QR that opens straight to that item's photos and review box. (Only products added here show up in the list below — a starter-catalog product will appear once you import it.)

Add a review yourself

For reviews that reached you on WhatsApp or Instagram: type it here and add the screenshot or photo. It is saved as approved and shown on the shop at once.

Reviews waiting for approval (0)

A review a customer submits doesn't show on the site until you approve it here. Approved reviews move to Manage reviews.

Approved reviews (0)

These show under their own product on the site. Use the star button to also feature a review in "What customers say" on the homepage.

Orders

Website orders are placed right on the shop. WhatsApp/Instagram orders are saved when a customer taps those links — check your WhatsApp or Instagram to confirm they actually sent the message.

Alerts (sound, banner and notification) only work while this admin page is open in a browser tab. Phones may pause it when the screen locks.

Promo codes

Customers type these at checkout (in the cart or on Buy Now). Changes reach the shop within a few seconds. Switch a code off to pause it without deleting it.

Move photos to Cloudinary

Uploads the 16 starter products' built-in photos to Cloudinary and gives you a lighter src/data/products.js to put on GitHub. This takes about 1.6 MB off your site, so it opens much faster.

Export Meta catalog

Downloads a CSV of all products for Facebook/Instagram: Commerce Manager → Catalog → Data sources → Upload. Re-download whenever you add products or change prices.

Admin list

Who can sign in to this admin panel. Owners are fixed and can't be removed; only an owner can add or remove other admins.
